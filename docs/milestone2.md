# Milestone 2 — implemented scheduling service

Milestone 2 was explicitly approved. The existing manager workspace, local data and skill-review features are retained. The backend is a separate FastAPI service and independently testable OR-Tools CP-SAT engine. No Strobbo integration, real data, public deployment, employee swap application, authentication or database migration is introduced.

## Input and constraints

The canonical `SchedulingInput` is frozen and versioned, with unique employee/skill/assignment IDs and validated references, seven Monday–Sunday dates, explicit employee employment rules and preferences, dated availability windows/absences, effective manager-verified qualifications, supervision needs, hard interval requirements, soft demand/budgets, prior history, baseline assignments/locks and adjacent-week assignments. Invalid calendar/timezone references, ambiguous/nonexistent local operating times, unknown qualifications, missing entitlement parameters and oversized inputs are rejected. Overlapping skill/headcount windows use the maximum applicable requirement, not addition.

Candidate shifts enumerate configured starts, durations and unpaid-break offsets. Default starts are hourly, coverage and break boundaries use 30-minute granularity, and default clock lengths are 4/6/8 hours. Existing baseline patterns are included even if not in the default template list. A feasible result is feasible for this **finite** candidate universe, not proof that every possible restaurant shift pattern was considered. Resource limits cap 4,000 candidates and 60,000 admissible employee-candidate pairs.

Binary x[e,s] selects candidate assignments. Confirmed availability must contain the entire clock window, including unpaid breaks. Pair pruning excludes absences, ineligible shift types, ineffective personal qualifications, invalid clock/break/continuous-work limits, and boundary rest conflicts. The CP model additionally enforces one shift per local day, individual weekly min/max paid units, maximum days, rolling seven-day paid maxima, consecutive-day limits and rest via non-overlapping UTC intervals extended by the individual rest duration. Fixed boundary shifts are included. Seven adjacent days may be supplied on either side; omitted boundaries are explicitly empty and warned about.

Per-interval qualified coverage uses effective qualification parameters, not employee titles or generic headcount. A trainee requires a distinct independent qualified supervisor on duty in every working interval. Binary pairings consume a shared supervisor capacity across all competency groups; trainees cannot supervise themselves or others. Unpaid breaks remove workers from coverage and supervision. Personal shift duties cannot be discharged by a colleague. Managers may configure ordinary duties to be performed while supervising; the current implemented policy permits this. Separate simultaneous-duty allocation, continuous mentorship and multiple simultaneous supervision needs per trainee remain unsupported and must not be inferred.

## Objectives and status

Budget-first, balanced and coverage-first use the documented experimental weights for uncovered desired person-intervals, absolute daily paid-hour deviation, preferred-range/time-window deviations, historical fulfillment disparity, opportunity-adjusted unpopular shifts, physical-assignment changes, and short shifts. Hard constraints are identical across strategies.

All denominators are fixed from the same input/candidate set. Integer coefficient scale is 1,000,000,000; fulfillment ratios use floor(100 × historical-plus-current assigned / desired reference). Metadata reports coefficient rounding error, bounds, runtime, seed and solver version. Fairness references are clipped to an individual availability-capacity **upper bound**, not a claim of jointly feasible entitlement. Legal minima remain hard and are never clipped away. Zero-reference/zero-opportunity cases are explicitly excluded from the relevant comparison. A high fairness score can coexist with general underallocation; per-person unmet preferences and coverage remain visible.

For larger models, a real CP-SAT warm start optimizes a smaller coverage/budget objective under all hard constraints, then fixes its core variables in a short solver pass to complete auxiliary values. That complete feasible solution hints the full weighted search. If the full search has no incumbent before its limit, the actual warm-start roster may be returned as FEASIBLE after independent validation, with phase statuses and a fallback warning. An OPTIMAL warm-start status does **not** prove optimality of the full objective. `solver_status` reports the weighted optimization phase; `status` describes the returned full-problem outcome. Every returned assignment comes from CP-SAT, never from a static example presented as optimization.

Runtime limits apply to solver phases together; preparation and independent validation add bounded overhead. Defaults are 15 wall-clock seconds and 2 deterministic units per strategy, one worker, seed 17. Settings and actual times are exposed in metadata. Alternatives may match; requesting three strategies is not a guarantee of three distinct rosters. These weights are hypotheses, not universal business optima.

INFEASIBLE results include exact preflight capacity facts where available and named sufficient assumption groups. In optimization settings OR-Tools may report a broad assumption set; those groups are not individually proven causes or a minimal explanation. UNKNOWN means no feasible incumbent was found within the limit, not infeasibility. MODEL_INVALID exposes a model diagnostic. No mandatory rules are silently relaxed.

## Independent verification and outputs

The validator has no OR-Tools imports. It rechecks assignment eligibility, breaks, real elapsed rest (including Amsterdam DST), week/boundary hour and day rules, locked physical assignments, mandatory staffing and skills, and supervision via independent maximum bipartite matching. Pairing outputs are reconstructed from actual returned assignments as a valid supervision proof; they are not a promise of one continuous mentor.

Metrics are recomputed from assignments, and full-phase objective components are checked against solver values. A failed invariant or mismatch yields VALIDATION_FAILED and withholds assignments. Outputs include per-day paid hours/budget deviations, headcount and qualified counts at each interval, explicit uncovered desired intervals, per-employee preferred/assigned hours and historical diagnostics, all normalized objective components, warnings and solver metadata. No target coverage percentage is fabricated when no desired targets exist; that metric is null.

The workspace adapter preserves the source revision and original baseline, converts existing skill rules/availability/absences and includes stored adjacent-week shifts. It exposes a fictional policy for parameters missing from the v2 workspace: default minimum entitlement zero, 5 days/week, 6 consecutive days, rolling maximum equal to the existing weekly maximum, and the existing prototype break policy. This is an explicit synthetic assumption requiring the request's confirmation, not a legal interpretation of zero-hour employment. Optional per-employee minimum overrides are supported. Ordinary workspace conversion supplies zero history and labels it absent; the dedicated fixture endpoint supplies explicitly fictional prior history.

## Scope remaining

Milestone 3: integrated generation/configuration UI, alternatives comparison, evidence explanations and selected-roster save/export flow. The manager calendar is not automatically replaced by API results. Existing manual edits still use the local TS validator; extending the manager editor to every additional backend employment parameter is part of that integration.

Milestone 4: employee requests, bilateral swaps, final manager approval, transactional fresh revalidation and application/outbox records.

Milestone 5: actual authenticated manager-only qualification changes, PostgreSQL/tenant isolation, effective rule management, immutable audit, privacy/security review and restricted pilot deployment. The current loopback guard and fictional-data flags are not authentication or a legal compliance certification. No employment-law conclusions or measured labour savings are claimed.

The manager still needs to confirm opening/closing windows and counts, personal versus team duties, supervision capacities/handovers/simultaneous duties, qualification verification and expiry policies, all working-time and entitlement rules, desired candidate shift shapes, and the experimental objective weights before any real pilot.
